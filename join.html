Questions

Do I have to leave my club?

No. You keep your membership. We play at partner clubs.

Does it cost anything?

Membership is free. NPL registration and court time work like they do on any league team, split among the players.

How are rosters picked?

Tryouts by division, then captains pick on level, results, availability and fit.

Not ready for a roster yet?

Join the community. Our tournaments and ladders are open to everyone, and tryouts come around every season.

Can I also play for my club's team?

That depends on the NPL's roster rules each season. We'll check with you before you commit.
