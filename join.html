FAQ

Do I have to leave my club?

No. Keep your membership; we play at partner clubs.

Can I also play on my club's team?

It depends on NPL roster rules for each season. We'll tell you before you commit.

How are rosters picked?

Division tryouts benchmarked to World Padel Rating (WPR), plus captain selection against published criteria.

I'm not elite yet. Is there a place for me?

Yes. Community members play our tournaments, ladders and events, and tryouts reopen every season.

What does "comped" mean?

We pay your tournament entry fee, up to the number of events in your tier, funded by our partners.

What's the refund policy?

Full refund until rosters are set; after that, prorated for documented injury.
