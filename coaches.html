Coaches

Coached by the best in the Northeast.

Crosscourt Coaches run team practices, coach at NPL matches and mentor rising players. They're backed by our racket-brand partners.

For players

  • Monthly team practice with a Crosscourt Coach
  • Discounted private lessons, at your own club
  • Coach-sponsored players: each coach picks a few players a season for free monthly mentoring

For coaches

  • Racket-brand sponsorship through our gear partners
  • A steady pipeline of motivated students
  • A featured coach profile and the Crosscourt Coach title
  • Practices run at your own club, so your club gets the bookings
