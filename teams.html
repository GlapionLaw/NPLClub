How selection works

  1. Request a tryout

    Tell us your division, your World Padel Rating (WPR) and your home club.

  2. Try out

    Division tryouts run by Crosscourt Coaches at partner clubs, benchmarked to WPR.

  3. Captain selection

    Captains pick rosters against published criteria: level, results, availability and chemistry.

We play every division at its true level. NPL places players by WPR, and we move players up before the league has to. That's how we keep it elite and keep it fair.

Mixed padel isn't an NPL division, so we run it ourselves at Crosscourt events.

We compete in the NPL's national team league, from city play to regionals and the national finals.
