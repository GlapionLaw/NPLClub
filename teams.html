How placement works

  1. Join as a Team or Competitor member

    Roster eligibility comes with membership.

  2. Level assessment

    A short session with a Crosscourt Coach at a partner club.

  3. Captain selection

    Captains build rosters by level, availability and chemistry. Published criteria, no politics.

We compete in the NPL's national team league, from city play to regionals and the national finals.
