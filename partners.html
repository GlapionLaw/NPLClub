Partners

Back the Northeast's padel team.

Crosscourt brings together top players from clubs across New York and Boston. Our partners keep it free to play, and their names go everywhere we compete.

Ways to partner

PartnershipSpotsWhat you get
Title Partner1"Crosscourt presented by" your brand, front of the kit, naming rights to our tournament
Founding Partner2–3Sleeve logo, site and newsletter placement, one hosted event, founding status for three seasons
Team Sponsor1 per teamBack of the kit, team naming ("Nighthawks presented by"), match-day posts
Event SponsorPer eventSignage, prize naming, a presence on site
Gear Partner1 per categoryOfficial racket, ball or apparel; coach ambassadors; demo days

Every partner gets an end-of-season report: members, clubs, reach and event turnout.

Who you reach

Competitive adults who train every week, travel for tournaments, and buy what their teammates play with. They come from clubs across the region, so one partnership reaches all of them.
