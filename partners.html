Partners

Put your brand on the fastest-growing sport in the Northeast.

Crosscourt reaches competitive, social adult players across clubs in New York, New Jersey, Connecticut and Boston. Crosscourt is free to players. Partners fund NPL registration, team kits, coaching and comped tournament entries, and their names ride on all of it.

Partnership tiers

TierAvailabilityWhat you get
Title Partner1"Crosscourt presented by you", front of jersey, naming of our tournament and comp program, all channels
Founding Partner2–3Sleeve logo, site and email placement, one hosted event, founding status locked for 3 seasons
Team Sponsor1 per teamBack of jersey, "Nighthawks powered by you", match-day posts
Event SponsorPer eventSignage, prize naming, on-site presence
Gear Partner1 per categoryOfficial racket, ball or apparel; coach ambassadors; member code and demo days

Every partner gets a season report: members, clubs reached, impressions and event attendance.

Who our members are

Competitive, social adults who train weekly, travel for tournaments and buy what their teammates use. They come from clubs across the region, so one partnership reaches all of them.
