About

One team. Every club.

Crosscourt is a padel team organization that isn't tied to any one facility. Our players keep their home clubs and play for Crosscourt in the NPL and in tournaments across the Northeast.

Your club is where you play. Crosscourt is who you play for.

Why club-neutral

The best teams aren't always found under one roof. A club-neutral team lets players build rosters by level and chemistry, travel to the best tournaments together, and get coaching from the best coaches in the region, wherever they teach.

Our mission: grow competitive amateur padel in the Northeast and give every player a team to play for.

For clubs: become a Home Club

We don't build courts; we fill them. Every Crosscourt practice, match and event is booked at a partner club.

  • Paid court hours for practices and NPL home matches
  • Your logo on our jersey and site
  • Full draws for the tournaments you host
  • Lessons booked with coaches at your club
