About

One team. Every club.

Crosscourt is a padel team without a home court, by design. Our players come from clubs across New York and Boston, keep their memberships, and play together in the NPL and at our own events.

Your club is where you play. Crosscourt is who you play for.

Why we exist

The best players aren't under one roof. We bring them together by level and chemistry, train them with great coaches, and take them wherever the best padel is.

League play is where we start. Our own tournaments, ladders and events are where we're headed.

For clubs

We don't build courts. We fill them.

  • Practices and home matches booked on your courts
  • Your logo on our kit and site
  • Players for the tournaments you host
  • Lessons with coaches who teach at your club
