How it works

  1. Join

    Membership is free. Our partners pay the way.

  2. Try out

    Tryouts fill eight elite NPL rosters per city: men's and women's, Development through Advance.

  3. Play for Crosscourt

    NPL matches, Crosscourt tournaments and practices, all hosted at our partner clubs.

Built with clubs, not against them

We don't build courts; we fill them. Every Crosscourt practice, match and event is booked at a partner club. You keep your membership, and your club gets the bookings.
